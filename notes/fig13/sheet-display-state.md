# Fig. 13 sheet: display and speed (state of the work, 20 November 2026)

Read this first after a compaction. It is kept current at every step that changes what is live or what is left.

## Aim
Make the Fig. 13 sheet (top of `public/simulations/momentum-spirographs.html`) look full and deliberate on a phone and a desktop,
run smoothly (measured), and give the reader large handles on the picture itself. Scope: the sheet only (the request of 20 November
2026 narrowed it to "only Figure 13, only the spirograph"); the stations, films and catch page are not touched.

## Rules that bind this work
- House design (`docs/styles.md`): Instrument Serif titles, IBM Plex Sans text, IBM Plex Mono labels; no new font.
- Nothing on the running figure reacts to the pointer merely passing over it; reading values is a tool switched on by a tap.
  Words change at most about 4 times a second.
- The physics stays as it is: RK4, h = 1/4500 s, the same strikes. Any faster code must give bit for bit the same states.
- The lecture note is generated by `tools/fig13/build_page.py`; never edit the article by hand.
- Branch `claude/fig13-spirograph-display`; merge `origin/main` before publishing; never merge `fig13-study-bundle`.
- After changes: docs, `python3 tools/inventory.py`, `python3 tools/i18n/build.py`, `npm run build`, browser test, push to `main`,
  check https://phyeinusul.com at phone size.

Where the time goes (profile, phone): the lecture note's KaTeX typesetting on load, about 2.5 s of main thread (sim.js);
per frame: RK4 of 100 pendulums at 4500 steps a second (about 38 %), native canvas work (46 %), the pinned trail drawn as
2700 separate strokes, a ring buffer kept with `splice`, the caption's `innerHTML` rewritten every frame.

## Plan (in order)
1. Speed: typeset the note only when it comes near (opt-in `data-tex="near"` in sim.js); pause the sheet when it is off screen;
   a fused RK4 with identical arithmetic; trail drawn in a few strokes; ring buffers; decay and colour in one pass; the overlay
   (axes, eye, pin) redrawn only when it changes; text 4 times a second.
2. Layout: compact head; on a phone the figure edge to edge directly under the title, a thumb bar of large buttons under it, the
   pinned pendulum next, then the lede; on a desktop the whole sheet and the pinned pendulum in the first screen.
3. Handles: drag the pin across the sheet (the probe pendulum is struck again at the strike under the finger, continuously);
   drag the sheet with a mouse to move the window; double tap to zoom in at a point; keys move the pin.
