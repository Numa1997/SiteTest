US4832968, description: "the preparation of a sealed can containing stout having in solution a mixture of nitrogen and
carbon dioxide gases, the former preferably being present to the extent of at least 1.5% vols/vol and typically in the
range 1.5% to 3.5% vols/vol and the carbon dioxide being present at a considerably lower level than the amount of carbon
dioxide which would normally be present in conventional, wholly carbonated, bottled or canned stout and typically in the
range 0.8 to 1.8 vols/vol (1.46 to 3.29 grams/liter). a definition of the term "vols/vol" ... "the number of volumes of
gas which are dissolved in a unit volume of the beverage, said volumes of gas being determined at a pressure of 760
millimeters of mercury and at a temperature of 15.6° C.""
US4832968: "the can is charged with approximately 440 milliliters of stout ... the stout 8 is supersaturated with the
mixed carbon dioxide and nitrogen gases, typically the carbon dioxide gas being present at 1.5 vols/vol (2.74
grams/liter) and the nitrogen gas being present at 2% vols/vol. the charging of the can 1 with the stout may be achieved
in conventional manner, that is under a counterpressure and at a temperature of approximately 0° C."
US4832968: pressure in headspaces "greater than atmospheric pressure, typically in the order of 25lbs per square inch
(1.72 bars)."
GB2183592: "the 2%vols/vol nitrogen gas necessary to achieve the required solution of mixed gas in the packaged stout is
derived from the liquid nitrogen dosing of the headspace in the can."
