"The yield stress and shear viscosity have been determined for a series of well-characterized, highly concentrated
oil-in-water emulsions (φ > 0.74). ... The yield stress is given by r0 = oφ13Y(φ)R32,
   [ tau_0 = (σ/R32) φ^(1/3) Y(φ) ]
where σ is the interfacial tension, φ is the volume fraction of the dispersed phase, R 32 is the surface-volume mean
drop radius, and Y(φ) is a function whose values are found to be significantly lower than those obtained in a previous
study. The shear viscosity of all emulsions is accurately described by ue = r0Y + 32(φ−0.73)uCa−12,
   [ eta_e = tau_0/gamma_dot + 32 (φ − 0.73) mu Ca^(-1/2) ]
... μ is the viscosity of the Newtonian continuous phase, and Ca ... is the capillary number, whose upper value was
about 10−4."
