--- p. 6334, Eq. (20), rigid (no-slip) interfaces = channel-dominated ---
"k(eps) = K1 L^2 eps,  K1 ≡ (3 delta Ic⊥)^-1   (20)   From numerical calculations for flow through a rigid, straight
channel with a scalloped-triangle cross section ... it follows that K1 ≈ 6.3 × 10^-3."

--- p. 6335, Eq. (22), mobile interfaces = node-dominated ---
"k(eps) = K1/2 L^2 eps^(1/2)   (22)";  measured for SDS foam: "we obtain K1/2 ≈ 2.3 × 10^-3"
(rho = 1 g cm^-3, mu = 0.01 g cm^-1 s^-1, L = 0.15 cm). Footnote 45: equivalent sphere radius "a ≈ 1.4L";
"the node-dominated model (mobile gas/liquid interfaces) predicts k(eps_fcc) ≈ 1.2 × 10^-3 L^2, and the
channel-dominated model (rigid gas/liquid interface) yields k(eps_fcc) ≈ 1.6 × 10^-3 L^2" with eps_fcc ≈ 0.26.
"both foam drainage models are strictly valid for dry foams only".
p. 6336: "Ic⊥ ≈ 310 for the channel-dominated theory".
