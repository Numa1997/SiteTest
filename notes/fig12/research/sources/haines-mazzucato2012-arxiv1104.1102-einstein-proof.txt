p.1 (abstract): "We present a mathematical proof of Einstein's formula for the effective viscosity of a dilute
suspension of rigid neutrally–buoyant spheres when the spheres are centered on the vertices of a cubic lattice. ...
Einstein's formula is recovered as a first-order asymptotic expansion of the effective viscosity in the volume fraction."
