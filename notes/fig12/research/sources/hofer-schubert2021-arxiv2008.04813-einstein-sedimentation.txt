p.1 (abstract): "It is known that the presence of the particles leads to an increase of the effective viscosity of the
suspension. By Einstein's formula this effect is of the order of the particle volume fraction φ. The disturbance of the
fluid flow responsible for this increase of viscosity is very singular (like |x|^−2)."
p.4: "On the other hand, it is known that the particles change the effective viscosity of the fluid. According to
Einstein's formula [Ein06], this effective viscosity is given by µeff = 1 + 5/2φ, to first order in the (local)
particle volume fraction φ."
