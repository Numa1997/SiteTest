--- p. 3154, dry limit ---
"For a random 3D isotropic system of flat interfaces, theoretical work suggests that G(1)/(sigma/R) ≈ 0.55 at
phi ≈ 1 [36]. Princen's measurements [8,19] of polydisperse emulsions qualitatively support these predictions;
he reported a divergence of Pi and G(1)/(sigma/R) ≈ 0.5."

--- p. 3159, fitted osmotic equation of state ---
"This is shown in Fig. 3 by the solid line, which is a fit to Eq. (5), choosing alpha = 2. With this choice, a
effective critical value of phi_c = 0.60(2) is obtained ... A similar behavior has been reported for polydisperse
emulsions [19], albeit with a considerably larger phi_c ≈ 0.72. ... Near phi_eff ≈ 1, our data show that Pi begins
to diverge, with Pi ≈ sigma/R at phi_eff ≈ 0.9"
"G'_p(phi) tracks the osmotic pressure, rising nearly linearly with phi_eff above the critical volume fraction
phi_c ≈ 0.64 corresponding to rcp." "we find that G'_p(phi) approaches 0.6 sigma/R"
