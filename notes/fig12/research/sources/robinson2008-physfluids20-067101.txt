p.067101-9: "With short depth of focus, one can estimate an areal bubble fraction, and with a measured mean bubble
radius, and the assumption of uniform bubble packing, it is straightforward to estimate the bubble volume fraction α.
Obviously, this changes with location and time, tending to zero at late times and deep position. From four different
images, low on the glass and early in the settling, we measured α = 0.237, 0.233, 0.242, and 0.266. At a higher
location, about a centimeter below the accumulating head, we found α = 0.315."
