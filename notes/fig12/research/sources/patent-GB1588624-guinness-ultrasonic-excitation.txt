Description: nitrogen "(a) it is relatively insoluble in fermented liquor such as beer, e.g. in the order of less than
5% by volume as compared with 100%"
Description: "stout where there is a major difference in quality between stout with a carbon dioxide content above
2.0 vols./vol. of beer as dispensed from a sealed package ... and similar stout containing dissolved nitrogen and with a
carbon dioxide content in the range of 0.8 to 1.8 vols./vol. of beer when dispensed in draught form from a keg
pressurised with nitrogen and carbon dioxide"
Description: "Conventionally the nitrogen and carbon dioxide release and the formation of very small enduring bubbles is
achieved by the draught beverage being subjected to cavitation as it suffers a pressure drop in passing through an
array of small orifices in a plate"
Description: "excitation ... (a) vibration of at least audio frequency applied externally to a container ... The
vibration mentioned at (a) above is preferably ultrasonic."
Description: nitrogen "preferably being present to the extent of at least 1.5% vols./vol. and typically in the range
1.5% to 3.5% vols./vol."
