--- p.2 (Sec. II, experiment) ---
"cylindrical Plexiglas container of internal radius R = 37.05 mm ... filled with a column of water of height H
which is surmounted by a foam layer of thickness h"
"The foam is produced when injecting a commercial hand soap (Hypron SA) through a nozzle with a grid with pore size
0.2 mm and has typical bubbles diameter d = 0.25 mm. The foam's liquid fraction is measured to be 25% once poured on
top of the water column (evaluated using the weight of a volume of foam). This large value relative to dry foams, is
due to capillary forces yielding an intake of water effectively enlarging the foam Plateau borders."
"thickness ranges from h = 2.5 mm to 6.5 mm"
"we measured the surface tension, gamma = 31.5 mN/m, of the soap solution. Its viscoelastic surface dilatational
modulus ... is found below resolution (0.1 mN/m), showing that the interface can be considered as mobile and that
free shear boundary conditions apply."
"Experiments are initiated by tilting the container of an angle psi and then releasing it"

--- p.3 ---
"The frequency of oscillations, 3.50 Hz, matches the theoretical inviscid prediction w0 = 1.84 sqrt(g/R) within 1%.
... This frequency is found unchanged when adding foam (0 < h < 6.5 mm)"
"first peak, systematically chosen to X0 = 5 mm"
"We found sigma = 0.0098, which exceeds the theoretical expression of the viscous damping rate by a factor 2."
"the damping rate is greater with foam ... but also evidently increases as the amplitude of oscillations decreases.
... the interface does not relax exponentially at a given rate, but instead its decay is progressively hastened."
Fig. 2 legend (p.3): SLM alpha = 0.01 (h=2.5 mm), 0.013 (h=3.3 mm), 0.016 (h=4 mm), 0.023 (h=5 mm), 0.030 (h=6.5 mm).
"Ff increases linearly with the foam thickness h, i.e. with the number of layers of oscillating bubbles."

--- p.4 (Sec. III A, model) ---
"we neglect foam's inertia relative to the one of water."
"the friction force per unit length of a wall Plateau border scaling as gamma Ca^(2/3), where Ca = mu X'/gamma ...
The total length of surface Plateau borders is given by their typical length, d, times the number of Plateau borders
in contact with the container that scales as 2 pi R h/d^2. Thus, the friction force acting at the wall in the foam
layer reads
  F_f ~ (h/d) R gamma^(1/3) mu^(2/3) X'|X'|^(-1/3),   (1)
... valid for a mobile interface"
Eq. (3): "X'' + w0^2 X = -2 sigma w0 X' - alpha w0^(4/3) R^(1/3) X'|X'|^(-1/3)"
p.5: "alpha ... scales as alpha ~ h d^-1 gamma^(1/3) mu^(2/3) rho^-1 R^(-5/3) g^(-2/3)."
Eq. (5): "X(t) = [X0^(1/3) - c R^(1/3) (alpha/sigma)(e^(sigma w0 t/3) - 1)]^3 e^(-sigma w0 t) cos(w0 t)"
"(w0 = 22.00 rad/s, sigma = 9.8 · 10^-3)" ; "the damping coefficient alpha increases linearly with the foam thickness h"
[the constant c is defined only in the supplementary material, not read]

--- p.6 ---
Eq. (6): "t* = (3/(sigma w0)) ln[1 + c^-1 (sigma/alpha(h)) (X0/R)^(1/3)]"
"A small foam layer (h = 2.5 mm) is sufficient to bring this time to a finite and relatively small value (t* ≃ 12 s).
Other values of t* corresponding to larger values of h are of the same order (the smallest is 5 s)"
