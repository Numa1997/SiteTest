--- Supplementary Appendix A, verbatim (void fraction by weighing) ---
"To measure the bubble volume-concentration, we poured Guinness into a 210-ml measuring cup that overflowed within 10 seconds after opening a can. The foam above the cup was removed with a spatula, and drops attached at the outer wall of the cup were wiped immediately. The measuring cup was placed on a digital weight scale, and then the weight of bubbly Guinness was measured. Finally, the bubble volume-concentration (see Fig. S1c) was estimated from the weight ratio of bubbly medium to the degassed Guinness liquid, subtracting the weight of the measuring cup."
