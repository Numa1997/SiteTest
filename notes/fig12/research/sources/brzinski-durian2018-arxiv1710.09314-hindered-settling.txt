p.1 (abstract): "the data fall onto two distinct branches, both of which are well-described by a hindered settling
function of the Richardson-Zaki form H(φ) = (1 − φ)^n but with different exponents: n = 5.6 ± 0.1 for Brownian systems
with Péclet number Pe < Pec, and n = 4.48 ± 0.04 for non-Brownian systems with Pe > Pec. The crossover Péclet number is
Pec ≈ 10^8, which is surprisingly large."
p.2: "In 1972 Batchelor predicted H(φ) = 1 − 6.55φ + O(φ²) [18]. In 1988 Brady & Durlofsky predicted
H(φ) = (1 − φ)³/(1 + 2φ) = 1 − 5φ + O(φ²)"
