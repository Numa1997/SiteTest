--- p. 1411, abstract ---
"While phi decreases toward phi_c = 0.635±0.01, G goes to zero, and the yield stress decreases by many orders of
magnitude with a quadratic behavior. The yield strain, which can be extrapolated to 0.18±0.02 at phi = 1, has a minimum
value of 0.045±0.010 at phi_c."

--- p. 1419, yield stress coefficient for foams ---
"In our case, sigma/R = 310 Pa ... we show the same form empirically used by Mason et al. (1996) for emulsions:
sigma_y ∝ (phi - phi_c)^2; the result is in excellent agreement with our data [Fig. 4(b)]. Especially for the numerical
coefficient: we found a value of 0.53 for the best fit on sigma_y [Fig. 4(b)] while it is 0.52 for emulsions."

--- p. 1419, shear modulus ---
"For monodisperse emulsions, it has been proposed that G behaves like phi(phi - phi_c), in units of sigma/R, with
phi_c = 0.63 [Mason et al. (1995)]. ... For monodisperse emulsions, G(phi = 1) = 0.6 in units of Laplace pressure; we
find here G(phi = 1) = 0.51. Both results agree with the prediction G(phi = 1) = 0.55"
