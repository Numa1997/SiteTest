--- p. 2099, Plateau-border area and the channel foam drainage equation ---
"For a foam consisting of a monodisperse collection of bubbles, on dimensional grounds, A = delta r_c^2; considering
the Plateau border cross section as the curved triangular area between three touching circles ..., the constant is
determined as delta = √3 − π/2."
"∂A/∂t + (rho g/eta) ∂A^2/∂z − (gamma delta^(1/2)/2eta) ∇·(A^(1/2)∇A) = 0,   (3)   where z is taken as positive
downward." [eta = effective viscosity that "hides much ignorance of the actual detailed flow"]
"If R is the 'effective' bubble radius, then A = c_N R^2 eps/(1 − eps) [8]"

--- p. 2099, steady (equilibrium) profile of a draining column ---
"A(z) = A0 [1 + (rho g A0^(1/2)/(gamma delta^(1/2))) (L − z)]^(-2)"   (A0 = A at the bottom, z = L)
Validity: dry foam, channel (no-slip-like) model, continuum scale >> bubble size.
