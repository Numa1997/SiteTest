--- p.8, Eqs. (15)-(18), force per unit length of meniscus ---
"f_sf^f = 3.84 gamma Ca^(2/3)   f_sf^r* = -1.1 gamma Ca^(2/3)   (15)
 f_sli^f = 3.75 gamma Ca^(2/3) + 0.47 gamma (l/r_m) Ca^(1/3)   f_sli^r* = -3.07 gamma Ca^(2/3) - 0.47 gamma (l/r_m) Ca^(1/3)  (16)
 f_rol^f = 4.84 gamma Ca^(2/3)   f_rol^r* = -1.38 gamma Ca^(2/3)  (17)
 ... the force per unit length exerted on a bubble or on a lamella by the wall is given by f = f^f - f^r. We define
 the parameters xi = (4.94; 6.82; 6.22) and zeta = (0; 0.94; 0) respectively for the stress free, sliding and rolling
 cases, so that  f = xi gamma Ca^(2/3) + zeta gamma (l/r_m) Ca^(1/3).  (18)"
"For a typical capillary number Ca = 10^-3, both terms are of the same order of magnitude if the meniscus size r_m is
10 times smaller than the bubble size l."
"[Denkov's model] leads to the prediction that the contribution of the sheared wetting film to the friction force
scales as gamma (l/r_m)^(1/2) Ca^(1/2). ... as shown in this paper, it does not apply to the friction between bubbles
and a solid, even in the incompressible interface limit."
--- p.12, lamella limit ---
"In the stress free case, this leads to the simple condition Ca < 0.26, or, for gamma = 30 10^-3 N/m and
eta = 10^-3 Pa·s, Um = 8 m/s. Above this maximal velocity, the lamella breaks."
