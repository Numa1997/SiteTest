# Fig. 12 lecture note: independent check

This checks the note `<article class="paper" id="paper">` in `public/simulations/beer-slosh.html` at commit 6b604fc
(sha256 09b3b574…, solver `public/scripts/stout-cart.js` sha256 ea408ee1…, glass `guinness-glass.js` cc61fc50…), 6 November 2026.
`notes/fig12/plan.md` was not read.

## Summary: what is wrong

1. **§5, push against gravity:** "Over a 25 mm bump in open loop it is 6.3 m/s², Fr = 0.64." This is wrong. The current solver gives
   3.58 m/s², so Fr = 0.36. The §13 table already says 3.58. The value 6.3 m/s² comes from the solver before the sliver-step fix:
   the old `World.advance` from commit 8e4a6f3 gives 6.28 m/s², with a 0.0192 ms step and |F_ℓx| up to 15.9 N.
2. **§6.6, "its spectrum peaks near v/L = 4 Hz":** this is wrong. One sine cycle lasting L/v = 0.25 s has its spectral peak at
   3.35 Hz (0.84 v/L). The computed open-loop push (a_gx, t < 0.8 s) peaks at 3.42 Hz. The real peak is even closer to the swing,
   so the conclusion only gets stronger.
3. **§4.6, "within 0.3 % of the cylinder of the surface radius":** this is wrong for the 2.5 mm grid. The note's own rounded
   factors give 3.4228, 3.4096 and 3.4064 Hz against 3.4207 Hz, that is +0.06 %, −0.32 % and **−0.42 %**. The unrounded factors give
   +0.04 %, −0.28 % and −0.38 %. The correct statement is "within 0.4 %".
4. **§13, "the speed loop holds it within 5 %"** (25 mm bump): this is slightly wrong. The speed ranges from 0.948 to 1.043 m/s,
   which is −5.2 % / +4.3 %.
5. **Figure 1 aria-label:** "the widest point 39.3 mm at 113 mm" is wrong. The widest point is at 116 mm (116–116.5 mm). The
   visible label and §2 both say 116. The value 113 is hard-coded in `tools/fig12/figures.cjs`.
6. **Figure 2 aria-label:**
   - "about plus and minus 1.2 m/s² in open loop" is wrong. The computed extremes are −1.24 and +0.84 m/s².
   - "with speed control it stays within about 4 mm" is wrong. The lean reaches 4.2 mm at about 1.95 s and 5.8 mm at 2.285 s;
     15 of 460 samples exceed 4 mm. The text of §8.3 ("under 5.8 mm") is right.
7. **Method description, smaller mismatches** (details in the last table):
   - §11.5 leaves out a rule for the liquid cells: wall-cut cells inherit liquid from open-face neighbours (`inheritWallPhi`).
   - §11.7 says "section 10.2 now hands over its impulse", but §10.2 describes a held force, not an impulse.
   - §6.4 sends the reader to "the integrator (section 11)" for the cart's energy residue, but §11 never describes the cart's
     integrator (symplectic Euler).
   - §10.2 says "substeps of at most 0.1 ms", but the code uses h/round(h/0.1 ms), which can slightly exceed 0.1 ms.

Everything else checks:
- All 49 numbered equations re-derived.
- All grid runs, study runs and closed-form numbers reproduced to the digits printed.
- Both figures regenerated byte-identical from recomputed data.
- The threads fingerprint matches in Node and in Chromium, with 1 thread and with 4.

Two kinds of item stay unverifiable:

- The machine-dependent speed figures. On this 4-core sandbox: 12.3 ms per quiet 5 ms step, 18.8 ms during a hard spill, and the
  caption shows 0.24 s simulated per s.

## How it was checked

- **Derivations:** SymPy 1.14 / mpmath (`derive.py`), symbolic where possible. Bessel integrals, the projection and the momentum
  integral were checked by quadrature.
- **Closed-form numbers:** recomputed independently in Python from the constants read from the code. The loop gains include the
  lags. `node tools/fig12/theory.cjs` was cross-checked against them.
- **Solver numbers:** rerun with the page's own `stout-cart.js` and `guinness-glass.js` in Node, using `tools/fig12/check.cjs`,
  `study.cjs`, `energy.cjs` and `same.cjs`, every grid, Δt = 5 ms. Five targeted extras were run in scratch copies:
  - viscosity off;
  - particle radius 1 spacing;
  - the cart step halved;
  - the pre-fix solver;
  - the swing fits.
- **Chromium (Playwright, deviceScaleFactor 1, `http://localhost:8790`, crossOriginIsolated, 4 threads):**
  - The page's constants were read from `window.Fig12.world`.
  - The page's own `build()` was used: "Open loop" or "Speed control", then "Start again", then `World.advance` for 2.3 s on the
    10 mm bump.
  - The threads fingerprint was computed single-thread and pooled.
  - The glass drawing was re-rendered at 4× and re-measured.
- **Agreement between engines:**
  - Node and Chromium give bit-identical `Fluid` runs: sha256 7747da757ea9d800 both ways.
  - Coupled `World` runs differ in the last bit of the cart's state (Math library differences). All printed numbers agree:
    lean 25.005 against 25.0 mm, a_x 1.2392 against 1.24 m/s², |A| 0.164276 against 0.164278.
- **Scratch:** all scripts and outputs are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-SiteTest/0b49df0b-40ae-5252-88af-013d7ae9b129/scratchpad/`.
  No repository file other than this one was changed.
