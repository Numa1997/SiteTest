## Summary

### Still wrong in the version checked

1. **§4.5, "(17a) is tested by the same runs … 25.1 and 6.9 mm; the solver's largest lean is 25.0 and 5.8 mm":** the test is
   wrong. The arithmetic is right: 2(β/ω)|â| = 25.07 and 6.92 mm, and 67.4 and 17.8 mm at 25 mm.
   - **What is wrong is the input.** |â| comes from `study.cjs`, which integrates a_gx over the whole 2.3 s run. After the jolt that
     includes the stout's own push on the free cart, the ripple of Example 4.2, which §8.3 itself names.
   - **The prediction drifts with the window.** Open loop: 22.1, 22.2, 23.3, 24.3, 25.1 mm for windows ending at 0.7, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0
     and 2.3 s. Speed control: 7.8 → 6.9 mm.
   - **With the jolt alone (t < 0.7 s),** (17a) predicts 22.1 mm and 7.8 mm, against the solver's 25.0 and 5.8 mm, that is −12 % and
     +34 %. At 25 mm it predicts 53.0 mm in open loop (60.7 computed).
   - **The quantity compared is not the one (17a) gives.** (17a) gives the mode-1 swing, but the comparison uses the largest total
     lean. The fitted mode-1 swing over 0.8–2.3 s is 18.4 mm (Example 4.2).
   - **What the runs support:** agreement in order of magnitude, not to 0.4 %.
2. **§5 recap, "the page computes 3.21–3.28 Hz on its grids":** this is wrong. The page offers 4 and 3 mm grids, which give 3.214 and
   3.263 Hz, so the range is 3.21–3.26 Hz. 3.277 Hz is the 2.5 mm check grid, which the page does not offer. §4.6 says 3.21–3.26 Hz,
   correctly.
3. **§14, "its ticks are every 10 cm and the label gives the distance travelled":** the label part is wrong (minor). The labels,
   every 0.5 m, give the road coordinate x (`stout-view.js`: `(m/10).toFixed(1) + ' m'`). The cart starts at x = 0.5 m, so a label
   reads 0.5 m more than the distance travelled. The ticks every 10 cm are right.

## How it was checked

- **Derivations:** SymPy 1.14 / mpmath, symbolic where possible. Bessel integrals, the projection, the momentum integral and (17a)
  were checked by quadrature or numerically.
- **Closed-form numbers:** recomputed independently in Python from constants read from the code. The loop gains include the lags.
  `tools/fig12/theory.cjs` was cross-checked against them.
- **Solver numbers:** rerun with the page's own `stout-cart.js` and `guinness-glass.js` in Node, using `tools/fig12/check.cjs`,
  `study.cjs`, `energy.cjs` and `same.cjs`, every grid, Δt = 5 ms. Targeted extras were run in scratch copies:
  - viscosity off;
  - particle radius 1 spacing;
  - the cart step halved;
  - the pre-fix solver from 8e4a6f3;
  - swing fits;
  - spectra;
  - windowed Fourier amplitudes.
- **Chromium (Playwright, deviceScaleFactor 1, `http://localhost:8790`, crossOriginIsolated, 4 threads):**
  - The constants were read from `window.Fig12.world`.
  - The page's own `build()` was used: "Open loop" or "Speed control", then "Start again", then `World.advance` for 2.3 s on the
    10 mm bump.
  - The threads fingerprint was computed single-thread and pooled.
  - The dimensions.com drawing was re-rendered at 4× and re-measured.
- **Agreement between engines:**
  - Node and Chromium give bit-identical `Fluid` runs: sha256 7747da757ea9d800.
  - Coupled `World` runs differ in the last bit of the cart's state (Math library differences between the two V8 builds). All
    printed numbers agree: lean 25.005 against 25.0 mm, a_x 1.2392 against 1.24, |â| 0.164276 against 0.164278.
- **Scratch:** all scripts and outputs are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-SiteTest/0b49df0b-40ae-5252-88af-013d7ae9b129/scratchpad/`.
