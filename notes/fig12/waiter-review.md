## Checked and correct

- (19): ∫xη dA = Σ πQ_nN_na_n for wall-normalised ψ_n (ψ_n(R) = 1). The sign is right: a > 0 raises the +x wall and shifts the centre of mass toward +x. The derivation holds for the tulip too, since it uses only surface integrals and the Steklov structure.
- (20) follows from (19) and (12) exactly (SymPy, `sy.py`). The sloshing masses m_n = ρπλQ²N/(1 + κ) agree with a Lagrangian derivation that includes the head's added mass.
- The page's A and B equal `controller.py`'s. The page's K matches SciPy's `solve_discrete_are` to 4.4 × 10⁻¹⁰ (relative, per element). The ZOH uses Van Loan's block exponential. Across 108 slider settings the Riccati iteration converges and agrees with SciPy to ≤ 1.3 × 10⁻⁸.
- Momentum with F = 0 (I_max = 0, a₁ = 5 mm, 5 s on the page's integrator): M ẋ + Σ c_n ȧ_n is constant to 2 × 10⁻¹⁷ kg·m/s.
- The y axis uses the sin copies correctly. Carries along +x, +y, −x and the diagonal all give 2.6255 mm and 1.453 s.
- Section 10's numbers reproduce on the page and in Python. M = 1.523 kg (stout 0.503 kg). Sloshing masses 79.0, 2.2 and 0.5 g; M_e = 1.442 kg. Waiter 2.63 mm, hand on the same path 7.98 mm, quick hand 127.8 mm. Arrival 1.45 s (page 1.453 s). 2.74 N, 0.228 A. Freeboard 160 − 137.539 − 18 = 4.46 mm.
- The T_s claim holds: at 60 ms the peak is 6.99 mm, past the 4.46 mm freeboard. Problem 2 has an answer: the head spills at Ω_r ≈ 4.2 rad/s (4.14 mm at Ω_r = 4, 5.94 mm at 5).
- Changing parameters mid-carry is safe. V 500 → 360 mL at 0.5 s into a carry stays finite and arrives in 1.85 s, with a peak of 1.2 mm. The sampling (tNext) holds the force for exactly T_s, because T_s is a whole number of 1 ms steps.
