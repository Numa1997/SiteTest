## Notes (none changes a verdict)

1. "Sizes span four decades of radius, twelve of mass": 1 um to 4 mm is log10(4000) = 3.60 decades of radius and 10.8 decades of mass. "Four" and "twelve" are rounded up (the grid has 289 bins at 8 per doubling, r_0 = 1 um to 4.096 mm).
2. "At the start 20.09 % of the water is in drops above 40 um": the continuous two-lognormal cloud has 19.74 % (analytic and quadrature agree); the 20.09 % is the value on the 8-bins-per-doubling grid (model `lognormalBins`: 20.0858 %), because the bin whose representative radius is 40.3 um carries the water from 39.7 um up. The text says "the thesis cloud as two lognormal modes", so it should say the figure is the gridded one (a 0.35 percentage point difference). Total water of the two modes 1.116 g/m3.
3. "Mass error -4.58e-7 ... exactly the water the exponential puts below the grid's first bin": the exponential has 4.58e-7 of its mass below the lower edge of bin 0 at 8 bins per doubling (3.53e-7, 4.20e-7, 4.58e-7, 4.79e-7 for 2, 4, 8, 16). So the number is the 8-bin value; the sentence reads as if it holds for all four resolutions.
4. The error table of the scheme (0.428, 0.150, 0.051, 0.018 and order 1.5) and the t50/t90 table are model runs, not equations; they were not re-run here.
5. The text calls (5.1) "an equation for the mean ... uncorrelated numbers (Gillespie 1972)": a statement about the stochastic model, tested in chapter 6, not an equation to derive.
