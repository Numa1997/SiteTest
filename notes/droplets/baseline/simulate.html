In-browser solver · stochastic collection equation

Simulate a cloud

Solve a prescribed homogeneous box using the SCE, 4 bins per doubling of mass and 1 s steps. The grid spans masses equivalent to 1 µm–2 mm water spheres. Two lognormal modes specify the initial spectrum; their geometric median and width are independent inputs. The displayed threshold is radius 40 µm. Read Theory chapters 9–11 for the numerical representation, phase assumptions and diagnostics.

r_g is the geometric median radius; σ_g is the geometric standard deviation. Changing width changes initial mass even at fixed number. Cold mode uses spherical ice, immediate freezing on contact, and the app’s event-based fragment law. A low mass residual does not validate those assumptions.

Set the cloud and press Run. A 30-minute warm cloud at 4 bins per doubling takes about a second; the cold cloud, with three pair types, a few seconds.

Built on the Bachelor thesis Collisional Ice Multiplication Processes in Clouds (N. Koudsie, Leipzig 2022).

Model: homogeneous collection box · SCE with two-bin remapping · empirical fall-speed closure and stored collision efficiencies. The 40 µm cutoff is a size diagnostic.
