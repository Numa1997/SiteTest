Retained numerical reference arrays for a prescribed warm spectrum and an adopted cold fragmentation model. The plotted fraction counts in-grid particles of radius at least 40 µm; it is a growth diagnostic, not a rainfall prediction. These parameter studies were not rerun in the explanatory revision. Use the theory for definitions, and Simulate for a new run.
