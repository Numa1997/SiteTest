## What holds (checked, not taken on trust)

- (L0.1) → (L0.2): correct. The partial fractions and the antiderivative F in (L0.4): correct (dF/dR − integrand = 0). The limit r → 0 of
  F(R) − F(R₀) is 1/R₀ − 1/R, which is (L0.3). Note that this limit is **not** in `L0_sympy.py`; I computed it myself.
- (L0.5): correct, and the total water N_c M + W is conserved along it exactly.
- Numbers: k = 1.2296 × 10⁸ m⁻¹ s⁻¹, τ_p(40 µm) = 20.08 ms, Re = 0.929, 3.137 and 14.52 (Stokes speed), Re = 1 at 40.99 µm,
  W = 0.6434 g/m³, R_m = 60.148 µm, F_c(0) = 0.2941, R(F_c = 0.5) = 47.74 µm and R(F_c = 0.9) = 58.07 µm. The open-cloud time 40 → 100 µm is
  9.9554 min, and the closed box with r ≪ R gives 3.9576 and 12.0426 min by plain quadrature. The finite-r closed box gives 2.927 and 9.192 min.
  All agree with the contract to every printed digit.
- The mean-field timing survives discreteness. A master equation with collector–droplet capture only, started monodisperse (the SCE restricted
  to this pair), gives t₅₀ = 2.926 min and t₉₀ = 9.184 min, against L0's 2.927 and 9.192 min.
- No equilibrium or variational tool is used as a law of motion. Terminal fall is a quasi-steady closure, justified by τ_p ≪ growth time.

|---|---|---|
| Motivated | **fail** | The open-cloud observable cannot be reached in the thesis cloud (it needs N_c ≪ 0.15 cm⁻³) and lies wholly outside the Stokes range; the snowball picture motivates the wrong growth law. |
| Honest | **fail** | "Cited: none" for the 1948–50 textbook model; "exact" on a 31–35 % approximation; three checks claimed that the cited files do not contain; "thermal effects: never". |
| Derived | **fail** | (L0.1)–(L0.5) are derived correctly (all re-derived, conservation exact), but the bounds in §7 are not derived, and prediction 2 contradicts a three-line derivation (R(∞) = 1/(1/R₀ − Φ)) and a master-equation run. |
| Checked | **pass (narrow)** | Every number in §6 reproduces to all printed digits by independent quadrature and the r → 0 limit holds; the misattributed checks (objection 12) and the wrong assumption numbers (7, 8, 15) must still be corrected. |
| Sourced | **fail** | No source for the model, E, coalescence, viscosity, ε or the thesis cloud; E has a published number (Long 1974: kernel ratio 0.50 at 40 µm) that the contract defers to L2. |
| Readable | **pass (narrow)** | Short, ordered and symbols defined; but the everyday picture misleads, and the closed form of (L0.5) lives only in a script. |
