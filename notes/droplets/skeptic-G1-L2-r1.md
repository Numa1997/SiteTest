## What I checked myself, and what came out right

- **(L2.1)–(L2.2).** I redid the scaling in sympy. St = τU/R and S = τg*/U = v(r)/U are right. Along a row of fixed R,
  St/S = U²/(gR) (a Froude number), so St and S grow together as r grows. That matters in objection 8.
- **Stream functions.** E⁴ψ = 0 for the Stokes ψ and E²ψ = 0 for the potential ψ (sympy). The Stokes ψ satisfies no-slip at q = 1. The potential ψ
  satisfies no-penetration and slips at u_θ = −(3/2) sin θ. The velocity components in `flowField` match the derivatives of ψ.
- **Flux formula.** Partners at the start plane move with a − S e_z, so the flux through a disc of radius b is
  ∫(a_z − S)2πx dx = 2π[ψ(b, −Z) − ½Sb²]. Dividing by π(1+p)²(1 − S) gives the contract's E. I checked this numerically
  by integrating the flux over the start disc directly. The two routes agree to 10⁻⁵ for every pair.
- **Interception limits.** 1 − 3/[2(1+p)] + 1/[2(1+p)³] = (3/2)p² − (7/2)p³ + … for Stokes flow, and 1 − (1+p)⁻³ = 3p − 6p² + … for potential flow. Both correct.
- **Critical Stokes numbers.** I used a separate formulation. Let G = η + St η' (gap plus coasting distance). Then G' = A(η) ≤ 0 exactly. A partner hits
  if and only if G becomes negative. I integrated this in ξ = ln η with scipy DOP853 (rtol 10⁻¹²). Results:
  potential flow **0.0833333**, creeping flow **1.2119420**, both at Z = 20 and Z = 100. These agree with the prototype and with
  Michael and Norey's 1.21194.
- **Independent trajectory integrator.** scipy `solve_ivp` (DOP853, rtol 10⁻¹¹ to 10⁻¹³, results unchanged across that range). It has a contact
  event and a closest-approach event. Fall speeds come from Beard, retyped from the dossier rather than copied from `beard.cjs`. Air: 10 °C, 850 hPa.

## Thresholds

- **Motivated: pass.** The windscreen-in-mist picture is the right mechanism (air carries light droplets round), and every rung ingredient is
  introduced for a reason.
- **Honest: fail.** The model is misnamed as superposition with a source that says otherwise (1). The "bracket" leaves out Hall's wake-capture values (6). The
  Langmuir comparisons skip the operating point or compare different quantities (3, 4). The gap to Hall is understated (12).
- **Derived: fail.** (L2.1)–(L2.2), the flux formula, the stream functions, the velocities and the interception limits all re-derive correctly. But the
  expected factor in prediction 1 is not derived and its threshold cannot fail (13). The 1/12 is half derived (10). The validity limit of the
  model's own superposition is not derived (7).
- **Checked: pass for the computation, not for the physics.** An independent integrator reproduces E to 2 × 10⁻⁴, and the critical Stokes numbers to
  7 digits. The closure that decides E(40 µm, 8 µm), Stokes flow at Re ≈ 1 with the 1/60 blend, is checked against no real flow field (2). I
  could not close that gap either: my Navier–Stokes solver failed.
- **Sourced: fail.** The superposition attribution contradicts the dossier (1). Langmuir's 60 is taken from a drag-curve analogy outside its use (2).
  Hall and Langmuir digits are OCR and unchecked against page images (17). Coalescence efficiency is unsourced and unstated (15b).
- **Readable: pass with minor fixes.** It is short and plain and the structure is clear. Undefined shorthand ("observable 2", "SCE", "old study")
  needs a line each (19).

Not checked by me: the Navier–Stokes-field efficiencies (solver unstable, stopped), the page images of Hall (1980) and Langmuir (1948) (no access),
and the old study's stored table and timings (outside my reading list).
