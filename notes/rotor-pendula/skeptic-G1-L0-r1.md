## What I checked and found right

- Geometry and signs. θ is measured from the outward radius in the rotor plane, gravity is −g ŷ, and the generator acts on θ̇ only. My own
  SymPy derivation (`hinge.py`, hinge axis parallel to the shaft) gives θ″ + (R − G sin φ)θ = −G cos φ to linear order, the same as the contract.
- Energy book L0.2, blade-angle form L0.3, the linear amplitude, κ* = |R − 1|, P* = m L g²/(4Ω|r_p − L|), Θ* = G/(√2|R − 1|), and the
  torque amplitude c*ΩΘ* = m g L/√2 = 0.1387 N m. I checked these by hand, and both scripts run with ALL PASS.
- Sol's stored rows. My own DOP853 integration reproduces all 18 nonlinear powers to 4 decimals (difference 0.0000 %). The
  time-invariant closed form at Sol's damping matches his linear rows to about 10⁻¹¹ relative.
- The parametric-term argument (d). My direct integration of the Hill (LTP) equation gives +11.617 % at 8 rpm 30 %, the same as the
  contract's 11.6 %. The ordering argument is right. Here is the split of the relative power correction at κ = R − 1, as a coefficient of
  1/Γ² (`shoot.py`, R = 5…300, 1/Γ → 0):

  | term kept | parametric alone | quadratic G θ² cos φ/2 alone | cubic R θ³/6 alone | all (full) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | coefficient | +0.15 … +0.22 | −0.25 … −0.39 | +0.06 … +0.12 | −0.037 … −0.052 |

  All three are second order and they do partly cancel. Keeping the parametric term alone is about 4 times worse than dropping it.
  The rejection is fair; it should show this table (objection 5).
- Other numbers: the share 7.2 × 10⁻⁵, the A5 drag numbers, the A6 friction fraction 0.005–0.73 %, the A8 factor 0.9924, the P2 formula
  2/(1 + Ω²/Ω₀²) and its mean shift +σ²/2, and the slider formula (if k_eff means k − mΩ²).

## Thresholds

- **Motivated: FAIL.** The swing analogy is not the mechanism (1). The claimed advantage, order tuning, is empty at √R = 1.4–17 (1). The
  order-1 tuning alternative is missing, and it is worth ×1.4–180 (2). The slider is rejected for the wrong reason (7).
- **Honest: FAIL.** P1 is presented as a risky prediction, but Sol's data in hand answer it (5). "Partly cancel" is asserted (5). Inherited
  choices and provisional Fermi inputs are not flagged (10, 14). Credit: the unopened SKF and NREL sources are flagged.
- **Derived: PASS.** L0.1–L0.6 and m g L/√2 follow from the model; I re-derived them. Gaps: the cancellation (5) and the flapwise claim (8).
- **Checked: FAIL.** The core numbers reproduce to 4 digits. But the A4, A7 and A9 numbers are wrong or missing (3, 4, 9), and the
  Ω → 0 and Γ → 1 limits are not run (12).
- **Sourced: FAIL.** The SKF μ and the NREL tilt are used as numbers before being opened (11, A8). The prior art on order-tuned rotating
  pendula and self-tuned rotational harvesters is not cited (2). The Fermi inputs are provisional (10).
- **Readable: PASS**, with the fixes in 17.
