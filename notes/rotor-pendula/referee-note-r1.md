## What is well explained

- The order (picture, then working, then check, then numbers) works. The reader always knows why a step is taken.

## Verdict

A strong second-year student could learn the core physics from chapters 1 and 2:
- how a rotating vertical frame turns gravity into a once-per-turn push on a centrifugally stiffened pendulum;

Learning stops being reliable in three places.

Fix the five errors in pictures and chapter 3, and add the short derivations. The note would then meet its own promise.
