## 2. The physics and what is already done and checked

A bob m = 0.1 kg on a rod L = 0.2 m hangs from a pivot inside a wind-turbine blade at radius r_p; the rotor turns at
Omega; a generator at the pivot brakes the relative swing (torque -c*thetadot) to power a blade sensor (~37.6 microW).
Question: can a chaotic double pendulum turn turbulent wind to its advantage over a single pendulum of equal mass
and reach? Who pays? What does it do to the blade? Groups: R = r_p/L, G = g/(Omega^2 L), Gamma = R/G,
kappa = c/(m L^2 Omega), eps = Omegadot/Omega^2. g = 9.80665 m/s^2 everywhere (Sol's value).

Scripts are in notes/rotor-pendula/derive/; run with python3 -I <name>.py (numpy, scipy, sympy). All print PASS/FAIL.
- scales.py, fermi.py: the scales and Fermi estimates (status.md page 1).
- L0_single.py (ALL PASS): Lagrangian with the rotor angle free; energy book d(T+V)/dt = tau_rot*phidot - c*thetadot^2
  (the rotor pays every joule); blade-angle form; kappa* = |R-1|; P* = m L g^2/(4 Omega |r_p - L|) = 57.09 mW at Sol's
  best mounting.
- L0_table.py (ALL PASS): closed form within 0.68 % of Sol's 18 nonlinear runs. Keeping the parametric term alone
  (Hill) overshoots by 11.6 %: inconsistent at second order.
- L0_r2.py (ALL PASS): tilted-hinge equation x'' + (R cos a - sin^2 a - G cos a sin phi) x = -G cos phi (resonance
  possible but needs alpha = 89.6 deg with sensitivity -301 per rad on large rotors); mean lean -G^2/(4R(R-1)).
- power_theorem.py, power_theorem_mc.py (ALL PASS): white-noise pivot shaking injects exactly (S/2)<b^T M^-1 b> <=
  (S/2) M_total; a double pendulum reaches M_total whenever its TOP rod is perpendicular to the shaking (corrects
  the brief). Monte Carlo within 0.22-0.65 %; chaotic double reaches only 0.55 of the ceiling.
- L1_double.py (ALL PASS): double pendulum, Lagrange (SymPy) = Newton in the blade frame to 6e-15; = Sol's equations.
- engine.py: vectorised 3-stage Radau IIA (order 5, L-stable); needed because the braked double is stiff.
  L1_engine_check.py (ALL PASS): observed order 4.9; = SciPy DOP853 to 6e-13; all 36 of Sol's runs to every digit
  (single 9.55-57.1 mW, double/single 0.635-0.735).
- L1_chaos.py: Sol's chaos group (R = 1, G = 3.065) with 16 starts per damping: chaos loses to the best regular orbit
