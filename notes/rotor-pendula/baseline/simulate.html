Run a pendulum on a rotor

Pick one of the study's mountings or set your own. The page integrates the equations of motion of Theory chapter 3 with fourth-order Runge–Kutta at a fixed step, leaves out the first turns while the start-up swing dies away, and averages the generator's power over the turns that follow. Everything is in SI units; the form uses rpm, grams and centimetres because they are easier to picture.
