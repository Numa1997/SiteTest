# Skeptic report, Gate 1, contract L2 (round 1)

Blind skeptic, 23 October 2026. I read only `contract-L2.md` and `derive/{turbulence,nrel5mw,L2_scales,L1_double,engine}.py` with `derive/data/`.
I ran `nrel5mw.py` and `L2_scales.py`. Both print ALL PASS and every number in §5 of the contract matches.
My own scripts are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-SiteTest/8b34d6df-5462-5589-b418-3710ebaaeab4/scratchpad/skeptic-L2/`:
`rotor_checks.py` (rotor sensitivity runs, 200 records each, seed 7 as in the contract), `pend_curve2.py` (pendulum power curve from
`engine.py`), `euler.py` (Euler-term harvest), `chiw.py` (χ(f) with weights that favour the outer blade).

Sources I opened myself:
- NREL/TP-500-38060 via OSTI purl 947422: §7.1 p. 19, Table 7-2, Table 9-1 and its text.
- TurbSim User's Guide 1.50 via OSTI purl 965520: eqs. 20–24 and 47–49, the ScaleIEC text and Table 3, PLExp Table 7.
- ROSCO `Cp_Ct_Cq.NREL5MW.txt`: downloaded, byte-identical to `derive/data/`.
- For the meaning of σ₁: arXiv 1904.07021 eq. 2.1 ("σ₁ is given by the 90% quantile"), the IEA Wind Task 42 report D3.7 and
  Ishihara et al. 2012 ("A study of the NTM in IEC 61400-1"). Both give mean σ = I_ref(0.75V + 3.8) and spread 1.4 I_ref, so
  3.8 + 1.28 × 1.4 = 5.6 is the 90 % quantile. I did not read the standard itself; it is paywalled.

What I checked and approve:
- The IEC Kaimal spectrum, σ₁, Λ₁ = 42 m, L_u = L_c = 340.2 m and the coherence. These match TurbSim eqs. 21–23, 47 and 49 exactly
  (5.67 × 60 m = 8.1 × 42 m).
- The disc pair-distance pdf and χ(f). My own Monte Carlo agrees: 0.791, 0.447, 0.066.
- The FFT normalisation. I checked the algebra, and the variance per bin is S df.
- The two-pass spin-up. 600 s is about 80 rotor time constants.
- J = 4.379 × 10⁷ kg m², from report Tables 2-2, 4-1 and 5-1.
- The torque-law constants, against Table 7-2.
- The drivetrain mode 0.6205 Hz, against Table 9-1.
- The L1 Euler terms. L1_double.py checks them two independent ways.
