## What it is

Project 05 ([fig11-double-inverted-pendulum.md](fig11-double-inverted-pendulum.md)) carried, until 7 November 2026, its own work on
liquids: water, glycerol, honey, a solved water tank, the energy budget of a swing-up and a controller told the flow. Its machine
cannot lift its rods in any liquid (note 13.7). Numa decided to move all of that to a new page with a new machine; project 05 keeps
vacuum and air.

## The machine, and why

Measured with the page's own code (`notes/fig11/project06/`):

- **Rods 3 kg each, 0.20 and 0.25 m.** Note 13.7, (48): a hanging rod must swing over the top at ω_need = 2√(f/I), and the liquid lets
  it turn at most at ω_t, where its drag torque equals its weight's pull. For project 05's outer rod ω_t/ω_need is 0.22, 0.17, 0.08 in
  water, glycerol, honey; for project 06's, 1.17, 0.98, 0.74 (`paddle.cjs`).
- **A 400 N motor**, and **a slim cart**, 0.40 × 0.06 × 0.10 m: of the motor's work in a full-force pump with the old box cart, 79–93 %
  heated the liquid round its face (`notes/fig11/project05/shoot.cjs`); the carriage's face is a quarter of the box's. Its drag keeps the
  flat-plate C_D = 1.28 (an upper bound for the rounded ends as drawn), r_eq = 0.0933 m for the Stokes part, κ_x = 0.1049 (the ellipsoid
  integral; 0.2876 for the box) and, in the solved tank, A₂ = 0.005876 m² from `notes/fig11/fluid-ns-handover/twoway/panel_addedmass.py`
  (400–3200 panels: 0.005852, 0.005865, 0.005871, 0.005874). The tank is as deep as the cart, 0.10 m (`TwoWay.setCart`, [twoway.md](../scripts/twoway.md)).
- **The planner's price of force in units of the motor's limit** (both pages): w_r = w_s = 0.02 × 60 N/u_max in (22), and 400
  Levenberg–Marquardt steps instead of 200. With the old fixed price (0.02 per newton) the planner stopped at gaps of 0.004–0.009 rad
  using half the motor for every heavy machine, even in vacuum; that was the reason for the earlier verdict "no swing-up in glycerol or
  honey". For 60 N nothing changes.

## The controller told the flow (note 13.8)

`flowEq(x, t, z0)`: Newton on the full equations at rest for (θ₁*, θ₂*, u*), (49); `linAt` the linearisation there; the balancer
u = sat(u* − K(s − s*)), (50), with s* re-solved each sample (`eqTrack`); the certificate and the planner about s*; `dragF` with the
flow (four Gauss points per rod, field frozen at the plan's start). Stored per sample as `cs_e1`, `cs_e2`, `cs_us`. In air (project 05)
the same code runs for the air flows.

Drag the cart and let go (`drag.cjs`, a river, rerun 7 November 2026 after the fixes below): while the cart is held the motor is off and
the short rods fall within a third of a second. At the release the balancer wins them back where it can (glycerol, 0.5 m/s: no rescue,
0.00° from the rest state −2.7°/−2.9° 23 s later); otherwise the supervisor calms them and swings them up about the flow's rest state
(water, 1 m/s: caught 6.0 s after the release, −1.14°/−1.24° from the rest state −4.6°/−5.0° 7.0 s after the catch; honey, 0.3 m/s:
caught 6.0 s after the release, −0.19°/−0.21° from −4.2°/−4.4° 6.8 s after the catch). The cart, released about 0.5 m out, drifts
during the swing-up and is held where it was caught.

**The certificate in a flow (fixed 7 November 2026).** `catchGen` stepped the planner's model `rhsF` (four Gauss points per rod)
about the rest state of the full equations (`flowEq`), which is not at rest in that model; near it W cannot fall, and the search could
return c* = 0 (internal wave, 0.1 m/s, 8 s after it was chosen), which made the supervisor rescue upright rods. `restOf(pC, z0)` now
solves the rest state of `rhsF` itself (Newton, like `flowEq`) and the certificate is taken about it: 17.3 there, as in still water
(17.0). In a steady river at 0.1 m/s c* stays 0.64 (unchanged by the fix).

**Deep flows ramp up.** Choosing a deep and slow flow (or changing the liquid, or Reset, while one is chosen) brings it up over 8 s,
U r(τ), r = 3τ² − 2τ³ (`fieldV`, `DEEP`, `RAMP`, `flowT0`); at the end the gains are redesigned once (`think()`, between frames).

The supervisor's guard (section 11) leaves a slow balancer in a liquid alone while the model shows it lowering W by a tenth in 4 s
**only if both rods are within 20° of their rest state** (`nearRest`, 7 November 2026): before, a spinning pair, which loses W to drag
too, was never rescued.

honey 0.01 m/s (the flow-speed slider of project 06 moves in hundredths). Choosing the flow (or changing the liquid while it is chosen)
