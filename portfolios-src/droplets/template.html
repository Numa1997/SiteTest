Droplet Collection

How a warm cloud of small droplets grows its first drizzle by gravitational collection, built one effect at a time: one drop sweeping a cloud, the drag that sets its speed, the air that steers droplets around it, the bookkeeping of a whole population, and the luck of the first drops. Every curve and every number on these pages comes from the model.
