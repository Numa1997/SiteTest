Panel 1 · the collection equation

A cloud in a box

300 droplets of about 8 µm and one collector of about 40 µm per cm³, 10 °C, 850 hPa. Choose the ingredients of the kernel and the bookkeeping, and solve 30 minutes of growth (4 bins per doubling of mass, 1 s steps). The dashed curves are the two stored reference runs (8 bins per doubling). Lecture notes, chapter 5.

Press Solve. A run takes a few seconds.
Panel 2 · collision efficiency

A droplet meets a collector

Paths of droplets around a collector, seen from the collector: the air rises past it, the droplets come from below. Paths that touch are drawn in wine. The efficiency is computed by the same solver that made the table of chapter 3.

The drawing updates when you release a slider.

Panel 3 · the stochastic process

Lucky drops

An exact simulation of every merger in a small volume of the same cloud (modes cut at three standard deviations, chapter 6). The chart shows when the first drop of 100 µm appeared in the stored runs, against the prediction (6.1) from the collection equation (dashed). Run your own volume to see one history.

Each run draws new drops and a new random history.
