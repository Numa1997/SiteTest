Fig. 13 · classical mechanics · chaos

Spirographs in Momentum Space.

A hundred double pendulums hang still, and each is given a different kick. The tip of each one draws its own curve: a tidy rosette where the motion is regular, a tangle where it is chaotic. Every curve is computed live from Hamilton's equations.

The tip paths are real positions; the grid only places each pendulum at its kick. Follow one of them: the dashboard (a wave becomes a circle, then the butterfly) · the flower hunt · films: from a kick to a butterfly, the bell, turning the paper, six pictures.
