Fig. 15 · quantum mechanics · 3D, computed on Kaggle GPUs

Resonant Tunnelling.

One thin barrier lets a slow electron through only now and then. Put a second barrier just behind it, and at one energy the wave piles up in the gap between them, rings there, and leaks out through the far side. Here an electron wave packet meets the pair in three dimensions: two walls, two discs, or two spherical shells. Every frame is from a full 3D solution of the Schrödinger equation, run on Kaggle's GPUs and stored. The page only plays the stored frames back.
